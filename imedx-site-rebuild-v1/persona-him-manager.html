HCS v2.0 • For the Health Information Manager

You own the gold standard, the rules and the release decision.

HCS v2.0 positions autonomy as a governed HIM process: measure performance on your coded episodes, tune against real corrections, retain visible control points and enable autonomy only where the evidence supports it.

What this means for HIM governance

Control stays with the Health Information team.

The HCS v2.0 campaign makes governance explicit instead of treating it as an invisible background process.

01

Define the Benchmark

Your organisation's coded episodes establish the standard against which AI performance is evaluated.

02

Classify Corrections

Coder corrections are treated as structured evidence that can be traced to the relevant coding decision.

03

Control the Rules

HIM ownership of coding guidance, rules and release decisions keeps autonomy accountable and auditable.

04

Release by Evidence

Automation expands only after benchmark and production evidence demonstrate that the defined casemix is ready.
