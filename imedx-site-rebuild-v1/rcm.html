Best Practice End-to-end

The revenue cycle depends on complete information at every stage.

01

Preadmission Documentation

Start of the medical record. Patient history, diagnosis, tests and procedures all need to be documented completely.

02

Progress Notes / Care Plans

Care plans and progress notes need to be well documented to carry out effective coding.

03

Complete Medical Records

Accurate coding depends on complete discharge summaries, pathology reports and operation reports.

04

Operation Reports

Legibility and specificity matter; typed reports can help coding accuracy and clarity.

05

Discharge Summaries

Timeliness and comprehensive content affect coding accuracy, the full episode of care, patient care and safety.

06

Training & Development of Billers

Expert knowledge of contract business rules and the balance between throughput and required accuracy.
