Best-practice end to end

The medical record must stay complete from preadmission through billing.

The current iMedX page highlights six points where documentation quality and process discipline affect coding, reimbursement and patient safety.

01

Preadmission Documentation

Patient history, diagnosis, tests and procedures need to be captured completely from the start of the record.

02

Progress Notes / Care Plans

Care plans and progress notes need formal, usable documentation to support effective coding.

03

Complete Medical Records

Timely coding depends on complete discharge summaries, pathology and operation reports.

04

Operation Reports

Legibility and specificity matter; typed reports can improve clarity for coders.

05

Discharge Summaries

Timeliness and completeness affect coding accuracy, the episode record, reimbursement and patient safety.

06

Training & Development of Billers

Billing teams need contract-rule knowledge and the right balance between throughput and accuracy.
