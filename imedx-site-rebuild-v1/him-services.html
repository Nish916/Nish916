Clinical Documentation Improvement

Understand. Enhance. Sustain.

iMedX describes its CDI approach as modular, practical and focused on building sustainable improvement around existing hospital programs.

CDI is the process of improving the quality of documentation in medical records. The current iMedX CDI model starts with understanding today's documentation practices, then enhances the program through aligned modules and supports ongoing sustainability through mentoring and support.

UnderstandAssess documentation effectiveness, establish a baseline and identify priority focus areas.
EnhanceDeliver modules aligned to the organisation's current CDI maturity and objectives.
SustainProvide mentoring and support intended to reduce program degradation and sustain culture change.
