# HCS v2.0 claim/evidence register — prototype control

Purpose: keep campaign copy evidence-first and prevent unverified quantitative claims from being published in the website prototype.

## Approved narrative supplied in stakeholder discussion
- "Beyond Accuracy Claims: A Casemix-by-Casemix Blueprint for Autonomous Clinical Coding."
- "Autonomy Without Evidence Is Risk. Autonomy With Proof Is Transformation."
- "Measured. Tuned. Governed. Proven."
- "On your own Hospital's casemix before autonomy is enabled."
- Performance should be proved on customer episodes before workflow autonomy is switched on.
- Primary campaign readers: HIM leaders and CFOs.

## Quantitative / technical claims requiring source confirmation before public use
Do not surface these on production pages until evidence owner and public-use approval are recorded:
- 40 + 20 validation method.
- Release threshold greater than 95%.
- Tuning toward 99%.
- 98.5% AI DRG accuracy.
- "a quarter faster coding".
- Exact number and definition of specialist agents.
- Exact number of engineering decisions / six-step correction loop.
- Exact production-scale autonomy gates.
- 40–60 episode CTA / single-DRG engagement size.

## Required evidence fields before release
For every quantitative claim capture:
1. claim text;
2. underlying data source;
3. sample/population and date;
4. calculation/method;
5. customer or internal validation owner;
6. caveat/limitations;
7. public-use approval;
8. page(s) where claim may appear.

The current prototype intentionally uses the evidence-first narrative while withholding the unverified figures above.
