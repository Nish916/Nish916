HCS v2.0 • For the CFO

Autonomy without evidence is a funding-integrity risk.

For finance leaders, the question is not whether AI can produce a code. It is whether performance has been measured on your hospital's casemix, governed before billing and proven before autonomy is enabled.

See the Evidence-First BlueprintExplore Revenue Cycle Management
What finance needs to know

Measured. Tuned. Governed. Proven.

The HCS v2.0 campaign reframes autonomy as a controlled progression tied to evidence rather than a headline accuracy claim.

01

Measured

Performance is evaluated at the coding decisions that determine the final episode and DRG outcome.

02

Tuned

Corrections and actual coding outcomes feed a controlled refinement process rather than an opaque generic model update.

03

Governed

Controls remain between AI output, coding confirmation, DRG confirmation and billing.

04

Proven

Autonomy expands only after performance has been validated on the hospital's own episodes and shown to remain stable.
