AI-Assisted Medical Transcription Services & AI-Integrated Technology For Hospitals

Clinical documentation solutions that are all about choice.

Designed to enable healthcare systems to elevate patient care, iMedX offers clinical documentation solutions that are all about choice, and meeting clinicians and health information management teams where they are at in their documentation creation needs.

The iMedX solution is powered by DeliverHealth’s eScription One (eSOne) platform – a market leading application coupled with iMedX’s market leading service, support, transcription, and delivery services to enable the end-to-end transcription service.

iMedX can provide a fully-outsourced, co-sourced and backlog/ overflow transcription service. The aim is to support you in delivering excellent patient care and to operate efficiently.
