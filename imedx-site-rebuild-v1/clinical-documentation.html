Security & Privacy

Privacy, confidentiality, integrity and availability.

iMedX states that its privacy and security policies are designed around these four core principles across its transcription and clinical coding services.

ConfidentialityAccess is limited to authorised users and the data required for their function.
PrivacyCollection and sharing of contracted client data remains controlled within defined processes.
IntegritySafeguarding accuracy and completeness through storage, processing and distribution.
AvailabilityEnsuring information is available to authorised users when and where required.
